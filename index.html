Two portals that help victims heal and caseworkers stay in touch

After an atrocity, a victim needs two things: a safe place to talk, and someone who keeps checking that they are protected and supported. Zen provides the first. Sahayak delivers the second.

For the Victim

Zen

A private, calm space where a victim can write, talk, and breathe without fear. It works directly on the user's device, ensuring complete privacy with zero unauthorized data sharing.

  • ✓ Private Journal: Write freely with real-time sentiment analysis that gently detects distressing thought patterns and helps reframe them.
  • ✓ Five Caring Companions: Tailored support personas including a friendly listener, cognitive reframe guide, legal protector, trauma healer, and custom confidant.
  • ✓ Know Your Rights: Plain-language legal guides and one-touch connections to free legal assistance (NALSA 15100) and Tele-MANAS (14416).
  • ✓ Calming Exercises: Interactive box-breathing visualizations, grounding tools, and curated psycho-educational libraries.
  • ✓ Multilingual Access: Speak, read, and reflect naturally in Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and English.

For the Caseworker

Sahayak

The unified administrative command center for district officers and caseworkers. It tracks case milestones, alerts response teams, and ensures no victim is left behind.

  • ✓ Automated Voice Check-Ins: Scheduled outbound IVR calls in regional dialects allowing victims to respond with a simple keypress without needing a smartphone.
  • ✓ Strict Consent-Driven Care: Victims directly share self-reported safety statuses; no assumptions or invasive telemetry are ever collected.
  • ✓ Proactive Case Continuity: Real-time triage flags non-responsive cases or emergency distress signals for immediate officer intervention.
  • ✓ District & Tehsil Geo-View: Granular jurisdiction mapping ensuring local rapid response units and witness protection officers take coordinated action.
  • ✓ Audit & Compliance Trail: Secure records for government welfare distribution, compensation tracking, and court compliance reports.

How They Work Together

Zen empowers the victim each day with unconditional privacy and psychological resilience. Sahayak guarantees institutional accountability and proactive outreach. Together, the victim is never alone, and the caseworkers are never left guessing.

1. Feel Safe The victim uses Zen on their personal device for private healing, emotional reframing, and knowing their legal protections.
2. Stay in Touch Sahayak initiates regular, low-friction check-in calls in the victim's mother tongue without requiring Internet or smartphones.
3. Rapid Help When distress is detected or calls go unanswered, caseworkers receive prioritized alerts to deploy protection officers immediately.
